## You were built for this

Step into nature and within minutes, cortisol begins to drop, the mind quietens, and the nervous system shifts into repair mode.

Time in nature does more than relax you; it restores you.

Picture yourself being in a forest.

The sweet scent of the earth.

The sound of leaves all around.

It feels good, doesn't it?

Being among trees lowers anxiety, sharpens memory, and boosts immunity.

Water revitalises, sunlight regulates sleep, and being outdoors actually makes social interactions easier.

Your body carries millions of years of adaptation to the natural environment.

To feel at home, free in your body and fiercely alive.

As distant as it may seem, that capacity is still within you.

Rewild is about waking it up.

By reminding you of *your* nature.

## Functional movement

Functional movement trains the patterns your body uses in real life.

Rather than isolating a single muscle, it works multiple muscle groups together, the way they actually need to function day to day.

As we age, we don't lose strength evenly.

We lose the ability to coordinate movement under load, stabilise joints, and respond fast enough when balance is threatened.

This is what leads to falls and a gradual narrowing of what the body feels capable of.

Functional movement in nature reverses this.

It keeps joints mobile, builds deep stabilising muscles, enhances proprioception, and trains the nervous system to respond well to the unexpected.

It helps you to move freely and confidently through life, for as long as possible.
